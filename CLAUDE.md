## Working on multi-part requests
- Before coding, write a numbered checklist of every requirement in my message (also small ones: tests, docs, migrations).
- Tick items only with evidence (file:line, test name, command output).
- Before the final reply, re-read my original message top to bottom, compare it against the checklist, and do anything missed.
- End with the checklist and status for each item. Say explicitly what was skipped or changed and why.
- Never use em dashes.
